Policy & Scale

Transparent conversion tables and rules used by this calculator.

GPA Formula

GPA = Σ(Grade Point × Credit) / Σ(Credit)
Displayed to 2 decimal places.

Raw Score Rounding Rule

Raw scores are rounded to the nearest integer with .5 rounding up before conversion.
Example: 92.5 → 93 → 4.00 (93–100 band)

DP Exclusions

Excluded from DP GPA: TOK, CAS, EE. They can be displayed but do not count in numerator/denominator.
