About This Calculator

This tool provides GPA estimates based on JCID's official conversion scales and rounding rules. For authoritative GPA records, refer to your official transcript.

Privacy & Performance

All calculations are performed locally in your browser. No data is stored, transmitted, or tracked.

Policy & Scale

GPA Formula

GPA = Σ(Grade Point × Credit) / Σ(Credit)
The GPA is calculated as a credit-weighted average and displayed to 2 decimal places.

Raw Score Rounding Rule

Raw scores are rounded to the nearest integer with .5 rounding up BEFORE conversion to grade points.
Example: 92.5 → 93 → 4.00 grade points (falls in 93–100 band)

DP Exclusions

The following course types are excluded from DP GPA calculation:
  • TOK (Theory of Knowledge)
  • CAS (Creativity, Activity, Service)
  • EE (Extended Essay)
These courses can be displayed in your table but do not count in the GPA denominator or numerator.
